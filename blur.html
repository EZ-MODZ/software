    Blur (2010)

    Blur is an exciting arcade racing game developed by Bizarre Creations and published by Activision. It combines realistic licensed cars with fast-paced racing and intense arcade action, creating a unique racing experience where speed and strategy are equally important.

    Race at High Speed

    Take control of more than 50 licensed cars and compete across a variety of real-world inspired locations. Race against up to 20 opponents while navigating sharp corners, jumps, shortcuts, and challenging tracks. Collect powerful abilities during races to gain an advantage over your rivals. Use speed boosts, defensive abilities and other power-ups at the right moment to overtake opponents and stay ahead.

    Game Modes

    Blur features a single-player campaign with numerous events and challenges, as well as multiplayer modes where players can compete against each other. The game supports competitive racing with up to 20 players online.

    Realistic Cars, Arcade Action

    The game combines detailed licensed vehicles with fast arcade-style gameplay. Each car has different characteristics, allowing players to choose vehicles that suit different tracks and racing styles.

    A Unique Racing Experience

    With its combination of realistic cars, fast races, power-ups, crashes, and competitive multiplayer, Blur delivers an action-packed racing experience that stands out from traditional racing games.
